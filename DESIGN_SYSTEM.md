# ALL THE SMOKE BBQ & CATERING — DESIGN SYSTEM & AGENT GUIDELINES

## 1. Brand Core & Scroll Rhythm
- **Aesthetic**: "Cast-Iron on Butcher Paper" (Dark culinary theater alternating with warm artisan utility breaks).
- **Dark Sections** (Hero, Packages, Pitmaster Reels, Footer): Deep charcoal and obsidian (`#0A0A0A`, `#121212`, `#18181B`).
- **Light Utility Sections** (Delivery Radius, Booking/Quote Forms): Warm butcher paper and parchment (`#F5F0EB`, `#FAF7F2`).

## 2. Master Color Tokens
- **Background Obsidian**: `#0A0A0A` / `#121212`
- **Background Butcher Paper**: `#F5F0EB` (Borders: `border-stone-300/70`)
- **Background Soft Parchment**: `#FAF7F2`
- **Card Obsidian**: `#141416` / `#18181B` (Borders: `border-zinc-800/80`)
- **Primary Headline (Dark Sections)**: Warm Bone Cream `#F4EFEA`
- **Primary Headline (Light Sections)**: Deep Espresso `#1C1917`
- **Eyebrows & Accents**: Antique Gold `#C8A97E`
- **Subheadings & Light Eyebrows**: Burnt Ember `#B45309`
- **Body Text (Dark)**: `#D8D2C6` / `text-stone-300`
- **Body Text (Light)**: `text-stone-600` / `text-stone-700`
- **Pricing / Highlights**: Antique Gold `#C8A97E` (Standard), Ember Orange `#FF8C38` (Featured)
- **FORBIDDEN COLORS**: Never use fluorescent construction yellow (`#FACC15`) or stark optical white (`#FFFFFF`) backgrounds for inputs/forms.

## 3. Typography & Casing Rules
- **Headlines (`<h1>`, `<h2>`, `<h3>`)**: Must ALWAYS use Title Case (e.g., "Elevated BBQ & Smokehouse Catering", "Spotlight Catering Packages"). STRICTLY FORBID all-caps screaming text.
- **Eyebrow Badges**: Centered, uppercase tracking-widest text-xs with star framing: `★ LABEL TEXT ★`.
- **CTAs & Buttons**: High-impact condensed display font (`font-display font-black tracking-wider uppercase`).

## 4. Master Components
- **Primary CTA Button**:
  `bg-gradient-to-b from-[#EA580C] to-[#9A3412] border border-[#FF8C38]/80 shadow-[0_0_24px_rgba(234,88,12,0.55)] font-display text-white uppercase`
- **Secondary / Ghost CTA**:
  `border border-[#C8A97E]/40 bg-zinc-900/60 font-display text-[#C8A97E] hover:border-amber-500 hover:text-white uppercase`
- **Form Input Fields**:
  `bg-[#FAF8F5] text-stone-900 placeholder:text-stone-400 border border-stone-300 focus:outline-none focus:border-[#EA580C] focus:ring-1 focus:ring-[#EA580C] rounded-xl h-12 px-4`
- **Cards**:
  Rounded 2xl corners (`rounded-2xl`), subtle drop shadow, and clean boundary strokes.
